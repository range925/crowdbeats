## 1. Verified Policy Strike Escalation Ladder
Crowdbeats does not penalize accounts on unverified raw reports. Penalties are issued exclusively upon verified policy violations evaluated by automated safety systems or human Trust & Safety officers:

| Level | Status | Enforcement Action | Restoration Condition |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Level 0** | `LEVEL_0_CLEAR` | Full platform privileges; active monetization. | Normal standing |
| **Level 1** | `LEVEL_1_WARNING` | Formal policy warning notice; content removed or corrected. | Acknowledged notice; 90-day clean period |
| **Level 2** | `LEVEL_2_MONETIZATION_RESTRICTED` | Monetization disabled across all sessions and campaigns; 30-day payout hold. | Completed appeal / 180-day remediation |
| **Level 3** | `LEVEL_3_SUSPENDED` | Account login suspended; public profile hidden; payouts frozen pending review. | Successful administrative appeal |
| **Level 4** | `LEVEL_4_TERMINATED` | Permanent account termination; Stripe Connect account deauthorized; banned. | Permanent / No reinstatement |

---

## 2. Severe Violation Immediate Escalation
Severe violations (child exploitation, violent extremism, deliberate payment fraud, commercial copyright piracy) immediately escalate to **Level 3** or **Level 4** without prior warnings.
