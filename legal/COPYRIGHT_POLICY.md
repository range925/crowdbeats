## 1. DMCA Notice & Takedown Procedure
Crowdbeats respects intellectual property rights and complies with the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (17 U.S.C. § 512). Copyright owners or authorized agents may submit infringement notices through our public complaint intake at `/legal/copyright-report`.

### Required Information for Valid Notices:
1. Physical or electronic signature of the copyright owner or authorized representative.
2. Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed.
3. Specific Crowdbeats URL (`https://crowdbeats.ai/...`) identifying the allegedly infringing material.
4. Sufficient contact information (name, physical address, email, phone number).
5. Good-faith statement that use of the material is not authorized by the copyright owner, agent, or law.
6. Statement under penalty of perjury that information in the notification is accurate.

---

## 2. Counter-Notification & Repeat Infringer Policy
- **Counter-Notices:** Creators whose material was removed may file a counter-notice if they believe the takedown resulted from mistake or misidentification.
- **Repeat Infringers:** In accordance with DMCA requirements, Crowdbeats terminates account privileges for repeat copyright infringers under our Repeat Offender Policy.
