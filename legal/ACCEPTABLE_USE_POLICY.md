## 1. Scope & Purpose
Crowdbeats is a live music engagement platform serving fans, solo musicians, bands, venues, and sponsors. This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") defines prohibited content, conduct, and monetization practices across all Crowdbeats services, live stage sessions, chat rooms, campaign descriptions, and public profile pages.

---

## 2. Strictly Prohibited Content & Activities

### 2.1 Adult & Sexually Explicit Content
Crowdbeats strictly prohibits uploading, streaming, promoting, or monetizing any form of sexually explicit or adult content, including:
- Nudity intended for sexual gratification or erotic display.
- Pornography, animated erotic media, or explicit sexual acts.
- Prostitution, escort services, or commercial sexual solicitation.
- Paid adult live streams, private sexual performances, or fetish content.
- Sexually suggestive or explicit paid content tiers.

### 2.2 Intellectual Property Infringement
All creators must hold the full legal rights or licenses for any music, audio, artwork, or video published or performed on Crowdbeats:
- Unauthorized copyrighted musical compositions, master sound recordings, or lyrics.
- Pirated music, leaked unreleased albums, or bootleg live concert recordings.
- Stolen artwork, album graphics, photographs, or video clips.
- Trademark infringement, deceptive branding, or rights-of-publicity violations.
- Unauthorized copyrighted media embedded in stage sessions or campaigns.

### 2.3 Violent Extremism, Terrorism & Hate Speech
Crowdbeats maintains a zero-tolerance policy against violent extremism and hate:
- Promotion, glorification, or recruitment on behalf of terrorist organizations or violent extremist groups.
- Direct threats, incitement of imminent violence, or encouragement of unlawful bodily harm.
- Targeted hate speech or degradation targeting individuals based on race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, disability, gender, gender identity, or sexual orientation.
- Protected-class harassment, bullying, doxxing, or swatting.

### 2.4 Fraud, Scams & Financial Abuse
- Fraudulent schemes, Ponzi/pyramid operations, or deceptive fundraising campaigns.
- Impersonation of other artists, public figures, charities, or Crowdbeats personnel.
- Sale or facilitation of illegal goods, controlled substances, weapons, or unlicensed services.
- Malware, phishing links, credential harvesting, or automated bot manipulation.
- Payment abuse, card testing, money laundering, or chargeback fraud.

---

## 3. Enforcement & Technical Intervention
Violations of this AUP trigger automated quarantine, content demonetization, immediate visibility restriction, suspension of tipping/payout privileges, and account termination under our Repeat Offender Policy.
