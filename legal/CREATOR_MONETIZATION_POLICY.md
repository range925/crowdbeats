## 1. Monetization Eligibility Requirements
To qualify for and maintain monetization privileges (receiving live performer tips, campaign backing, or ticket revenue), a creator must satisfy all of the following conditions:
1. **Verified Account & Persona:** Must hold an active Solo Musician or Band account in good standing.
2. **Legal & Policy Acceptance:** Must explicitly accept the Terms of Service, Acceptable Use Policy, and this Creator Monetization Policy with server-timestamped records.
3. **Stripe Connect Express Onboarding:** Must successfully complete Stripe Connect Express onboarding with verified identity, bank account details, and active `charges_enabled` status.
4. **Canonical Public Profile URL:** Must maintain an active canonical profile URL (`https://crowdbeats.ai/artist/{slug}` or `/band/{slug}`) submitted to Stripe.
5. **No Moderation Holds or Suspensions:** Must be free of active Trust & Safety compliance holds, unresolved copyright takedowns, or severe repeat offender strikes.

---

## 2. Server-Authoritative Fee Structure & Payouts
- **Platform Fee:** Crowdbeats deducts a server-governed platform technology fee (e.g. 5.00% / 500 bps) from eligible gross transactions as disclosed at checkout.
- **Financial System of Record:** Stripe serves as the sole financial system of record. Crowdbeats does not operate a custodial stored-value wallet.
- **Independent Demonetization:** Crowdbeats reserves the right to disable monetization on specific content items or across a creator account while allowing non-monetized public discovery where appropriate.
