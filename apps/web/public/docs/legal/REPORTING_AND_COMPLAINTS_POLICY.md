# Crowdbeats Content Reporting & Abuse Handling Policy

**Effective Date:** August 25, 2026  
**Version:** 2026-08-25  
**Canonical URL:** `https://crowdbeats.ai/legal/report-abuse`

---

## 1. Overview
Crowdbeats provides transparent, accessible mechanisms for fans, creators, venue managers, and members of the public to report content or conduct that violates our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms) or [Acceptable Use Policy](/legal/aup).

---

## 2. How to Report Content or Users
Reports can be submitted through two official channels:

### 2.1 In-App Reporting
- Tap or click the **Flag / Report** button on any artist profile, song request, live chat message, or tipping feed entry.
- Select the applicable reason category (e.g., Harassment, Inappropriate Media, Impersonation, Copyright, Spam).
- Submit supporting notes and screenshots.

### 2.2 Direct Web & Email Portal
- Visit our public reporting portal: [`https://crowdbeats.ai/legal/report-abuse`](/legal/report-abuse).
- Email: `trust-safety@crowdbeats.ai`.

---

## 3. Categories of Reports
1. **Critical Safety Violations:** CSAM/CSAE, imminent violence, suicide or self-harm encouragement, terroristic threats.
2. **Harmful Content:** Hate speech, targeted harassment, non-consensual imagery, doxxing.
3. **Financial & Account Abuse:** Stolen credit cards, fraudulent campaigns, extortion, unauthorized commercial solicitation.
4. **Intellectual Property:** Copyright and trademark claims (routed to [DMCA team](/legal/dmca)).

---

## 4. Moderation SLAs & Review Process
- **Critical Safety SLA:** Initial triage within **1 hour** of submission. Immediate automated quarantine and escalation to senior trust & safety personnel.
- **General Policy SLA:** Human moderator review within **24 hours** of submission.
- **Investigation Actions:** Moderators may issue warnings, remove content, restrict tipping, place payout holds, or ban accounts.
- **Reporter Updates:** Reporters receive an automated ticket acknowledgement and outcome notification where legally permissible.

---

## 5. Appeals Process
If an account or piece of content was moderated in error, the affected creator or user may submit an appeal within **14 days** of the enforcement notice:
- Email: `appeals@crowdbeats.ai`.
- Include: Account UID, case reference ID, and detailed factual explanation supporting reinstatement.
- Appeals are reviewed by a supervisor not involved in the original determination.
