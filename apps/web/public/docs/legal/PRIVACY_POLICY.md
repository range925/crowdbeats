## 2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT & WHY WE COLLECT IT
We collect only essential data needed to deliver free artist discovery and voluntary live tipping:

1. **Free Account Information:** Email address, display name, username, and authentication tokens (Google, Apple, or email login) to manage your profile and favorites.
2. **Ephemeral Local Geolocation (Optional):** Used in real time solely to display nearby live musicians, bands, and venues on our interactive discovery map. Geolocation calculations are processed locally on your device and are never sold or permanently logged to surveillance databases.
3. **Payment Tokens for Voluntary Tips:** Tokenized customer and payment references processed securely via PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant processors (Stripe, Inc., Apple Pay, Google Pay). Crowdbeats LLC **never** receives, handles, or stores raw credit card numbers or banking PINs.
4. **Performance & Interaction Logs:** Song requests, live session check-ins, and voluntary tip allocations recorded on our immutable zero-sum ledger.

## 3. YOUR CCPA / CPRA RIGHTS (CALIFORNIA RESIDENTS)
If you reside in California, you hold explicit statutory rights under the CCPA/CPRA:

- **Right to Know & Access:** Request full disclosure of the personal data categories collected about you over the preceding 12 months, accessible instantly via your [Account Statement PDF](/statement/pdf).
- **Right to Delete:** Request permanent erasure of your personal data from all active databases and backup systems.
- **Right to Correct:** Request correction of inaccurate profile information.
- **Right to Opt-Out (Do Not Sell/Share):** **Crowdbeats LLC DOES NOT SELL OR SHARE PERSONAL INFORMATION, TELEMETRY, OR FINANCIAL DATA TO THIRD PARTIES.**
- **Right to Limit Sensitive Data:** Restrict use of sensitive data (such as precise geolocation) strictly to necessary platform discovery functions.
- **Right to Non-Discrimination:** You will never receive diminished service or altered terms for exercising your statutory privacy rights.

To submit a CCPA request, email `privacy@crowdbeats.ai` or visit our [Help & Support Center](/legal/support).

## 4. YOUR GDPR RIGHTS (EUROPEAN UNION / EEA RESIDENTS)
For users in the European Union, European Economic Area, or United Kingdom, processing complies with GDPR Articles 15 through 22:

1. **Right of Access (Art. 15):** Obtain confirmation and structured copies of personal data processed.
2. **Right to Rectification (Art. 16):** Update or correct inaccurate personal records.
3. **Right to Erasure / "Right to be Forgotten" (Art. 17):** Request total deletion when data is no longer necessary.
4. **Right to Restriction of Processing (Art. 18):** Limit processing during active dispute inquiries.
5. **Right to Data Portability (Art. 20):** Export your personal profile and tip history in a standard PDF, CSV, or JSON format.
6. **Right to Object (Art. 21):** Object to processing based on legitimate interests.
7. **Automated Decision-Making (Art. 22):** Right not to be subject to legal decisions based solely on automated algorithmic processing.
