# Crowdbeats V2 — Privacy Policy: Stripe Alignment Gap Analysis

**Document Status:** Privacy & Data Protection Regulatory Audit  
**Authoritative Comparison Standards:**
- Stripe Privacy Policy (https://stripe.com/privacy)
- California Consumer Privacy Act / CPRA (Cal. Civ. Code § 1798.100 et seq.)

---

## 1. Identified Gaps & Required Additions

| Topic | Current Baseline Language | Stripe & CPRA Requirement / Gap | Action Required in Redline |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Stripe Privacy Roles** | Generic mention that Stripe processes data. | Must clarify that Stripe acts as an independent controller for fraud prevention, credit risk, and regulatory identity verification. | Update Section 4 with clear Stripe privacy role disclosures. |
| **Location Data** | Live session check-ins mentioned generally. | Must explicitly distinguish between approximate location (Stage Radar) and precise GPS, detailing opt-in consent mechanisms. | Add Section 2.3 on Geolocation & Live Stage Radar. |
| **AI & Automated Features** | Not disclosed in baseline privacy policy. | Must disclose AI profile bio formatting, toxicity screening, and human-in-the-loop safeguards. | Add Section 3.2 on Generative AI & Moderation Assistance. |
| **Connected Account Data** | Brief mention of Connected Account ID. | Must detail SSN/EIN, business documentation, and bank routing information collected directly by Stripe during KYC onboarding. | Add Section 2.2 detailing Stripe KYC data collection. |
| **Data Retention Schedule** | 7-year blanket financial statement. | Must provide a granular retention schedule across account profiles, messages, KYC status, and security audit logs. | Add Section 5 granular retention schedule. |
