## 1. Section-by-Section Baseline Inventory

1. **Section 1: Introduction**
   - General privacy commitment, references GDPR & CCPA/CPRA.
2. **Section 2: Information We Collect**
   - Account info (name, email, avatar, persona).
   - Financial data (card numbers handled by Stripe, tokenized references stored).
   - Session & engagement data (song requests, live check-ins, tips).
   - Device & telemetry (IP hash, browser type, crash reports).
3. **Section 3: How We Use Your Information**
   - Service delivery, Stripe payments, trust & safety, statutory tax compliance.
4. **Section 4: Third-Party Data Sharing**
   - Stripe for payments, GCP/Firebase for hosting.
   - Affirmative pledge: Crowdbeats does not sell personal data.
5. **Section 5: Data Security & Retention**
   - TLS 1.3 transit encryption, AES-256 at rest, 7-year financial record retention.
6. **Section 6: Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA)**
   - Access, correction, erasure, marketing opt-out, `privacy@crowdbeats.ai`.
