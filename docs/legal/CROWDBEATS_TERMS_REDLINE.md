3. `[+] Merchant of Record Disclosures:`

   - `[+] For crowdfunding campaign contributions, the campaign creator/band is the merchant of record for the project reward.`
4. `[+] Platform Fees & 5% Take-Rate:`
   - `[+] Crowdbeats assesses a 5.00% (500 basis points) platform technology fee on gross tip amounts, deducted synchronously upon charge authorization.`

5. `[+] Fan Refunds & Dispute Balances:`
