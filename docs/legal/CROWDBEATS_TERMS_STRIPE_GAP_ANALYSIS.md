# Crowdbeats V2 — Terms of Service: Stripe Alignment Gap Analysis

**Document Status:** Legal & Regulatory Audit  
**Authoritative Comparison Standards:**
- Stripe Services Agreement (US) (https://stripe.com/legal/ssa)
- Stripe Connected Account Agreement (https://stripe.com/legal/connect-account)
- Stripe Marketplace Guidance (https://docs.stripe.com/connect)

---

## 1. Identified Gaps & Required Additions

| Topic | Current Baseline Language | Stripe Requirement / Gap | Action Required in Redline |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Connected Accounts** | General statement that Stripe processes payouts. | Connected account terms must be accepted by reference to Stripe Connected Account Agreement. | Add explicit linked acceptance and obligations for creators and band owners. |
| **Merchant of Record** | Not expressly articulated per transaction type. | Destination charge model requires assigning merchant of record for tips (performer) and campaigns (campaign owner). | Explicitly define MOR per charge model in Section 4. |
| **Platform Fees** | Mentions 5% fee in contracts, but not fully detailed in TOS. | Must disclose calculation method (500 bps), deduction timing, and non-refundable nature of processing fees. | Disclose upfront 5% fee transparency before authorization. |
| **Refunds & Disputes** | Minimal mention of disputes. | Must detail 24-hour fan refund window, dispute balance recovery from Connected Account, and band split reversal. | Add comprehensive Section 4.3 on refunds, chargebacks, and reserves. |
| **Restricted Activities** | Generic AUP reference. | Must incorporate Stripe Restricted Businesses standards (anti-money laundering, card abuse, misleading campaigns). | Explicitly cross-link and summarize operational restrictions. |
| **Taxes (1099-K)** | Not mentioned in TOS body. | Must clarify that Stripe or Crowdbeats issues statutory 1099-K tax reporting to creators meeting IRS thresholds. | Add Section 5 on tax reporting responsibilities. |
