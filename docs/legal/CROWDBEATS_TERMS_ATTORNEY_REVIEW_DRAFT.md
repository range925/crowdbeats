## 3. Platform Role & Non-Custodial Architecture
Crowdbeats operates an interactive engagement technology platform connecting fans, musicians, bands, and venues. **Crowdbeats is not a bank, money transmitter, or custodial wallet.** Crowdbeats does not custody user funds or issue stored-value balances.

## 4. Payment Processing & Stripe Connect Services
1. **Stripe Processing Integration:** All payment processing, card vaulting, identity verification, and payout distributions are handled by **Stripe, Inc.** and its licensed financial entities.
2. **Connected Account Agreement:** Performing artists, band entities, and payout recipients must maintain a valid Stripe Connected Account and agree to the [Stripe Connected Account Agreement](https://stripe.com/legal/connect-account) and [Stripe Services Agreement](https://stripe.com/legal/ssa).
3. **Merchant of Record:**
   - For live stage voluntary fan tips, the performing creator is the merchant of record for the underlying entertainment performance.
   - For crowdfunding campaign contributions, the campaign creator/band entity is the merchant of record for project deliverables and fulfillment.
4. **Platform Fees (5% Take-Rate):** Crowdbeats assesses a 5.00% platform technology fee on gross voluntary tips, calculated and deducted synchronously upon PaymentIntent authorization.
5. **Refunds, Chargebacks & Reserve Holds:**
   - Fans may request voluntary tip refunds within twenty-four (24) hours of session completion.
   - In the event of a disputed transaction or chargeback, funds will be recovered from the creator or band Connected Account balance, and associated automated band split disbursements will be reversed.
