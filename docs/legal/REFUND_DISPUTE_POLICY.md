# Crowdbeats Refund & Tip Dispute Policy

**Effective Date:** August 21, 2026  
**Jurisdiction:** State of California, United States of America  
**Entity:** Crowdbeats LLC ("Company", "we", "us", or "our")  
**Platform Model:** 100% Free Platform Access (Voluntary Live Tipping)  
**Canonical URL:** `https://crowdbeats.ai/legal/refunds`  
**Version:** 2026-08-21-v3  

---

### 🌟 Plain-English Summary (User-Friendly Overview)
- **Voluntary Fan Tips:** Tips sent to live musicians and bands are voluntary gratuities that go directly into creator Stripe accounts in real time.
- **Tips Are Non-Refundable:** Because funds are disbursed immediately to artists, tips are non-refundable once sent.
- **14-Day Safety Net for Technical Errors:** If you experienced an accidental duplicate charge or technical glitch, you can open a dispute ticket in our [Help & Support Center](/legal/support) within 14 days, and our team will make it right.

---

## 1. Nature of Tipping Transactions
Tips sent on Crowdbeats represent voluntary, non-reciprocal gratuities paid by fans directly to musical performers and creators. Tips do not represent purchases of commercial goods or contractual services. There are no platform subscription fees.

---

## 2. General Policy: Finality of Voluntary Tips
Because tips are immediately disbursed to verified creator Stripe Connected Accounts:
- **General Rule:** Tips are final and non-refundable once the live session has concluded and the transaction is settled.
- **Accidental or Duplicate Transactions:** Fans who believe a tip was processed due to a technical glitch, duplicate charge, or unauthorized account access must submit a refund dispute within **14 calendar days** of the transaction date.

---

## 3. Eligible Dispute Circumstances
Crowdbeats will review and process tip disputes in the following verified scenarios:
1. **System Duplicate Charge:** A technical glitch caused multiple identical charges for a single tip intent.
2. **Unauthorized Card Usage:** Confirmed fraudulent use of a stolen payment card prior to creator payout release.
3. **Session Cancellation / Force Majeure:** The creator did not perform, and the scheduled live session was canceled before starting.
4. **Creator Misrepresentation:** The receiving profile was determined to be an impersonator or violator of the [Acceptable Use Policy](/legal/aup).

---

## 4. How to Submit a Dispute Request
1. **Online Support Portal:** Visit our [Help & Support Center](/legal/support) and select the "Tipping & Stripe Payments" category.
2. **Email Support:** Email `support@crowdbeats.ai` with the subject **"Tip Dispute: [Transaction ID]"**.
3. **Review Window:** Our support and operations team reviews dispute requests within **3 business days**. Approved adjustments are credited back to the original payment method via Stripe.

---

## 5. Chargebacks & Payment Network Disputes
If a fan files a payment dispute directly with their card issuer:
- The transaction enters formal dispute status in Stripe.
- Crowdbeats provides transaction metadata, session participation logs, and IP records to the card network.
- Excessive unwarranted chargebacks may result in account suspension for payment fraud.
