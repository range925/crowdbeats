## 1. Introduction
Welcome to Crowdbeats. This Privacy Policy details our practices regarding the collection, processing, and protection of personal data when using our live performance discovery and creator monetization services.

---

## 2. Information We Collect
1. **Account & Persona Data:** Display name, email, avatar image, and persona role.
2. `[+] Payment & Stripe Connected Account Data:`
   - `[+] Cardholder payment data is collected directly by Stripe, Inc. Crowdbeats never receives complete PAN card numbers or CVC security codes.`
   - `[+] For performing artists and band payout recipients, Stripe collects identity information (legal name, date of birth, SSN/EIN, government ID, and bank account details) during KYC verification.`
3. `[+] Geolocation & Live Radar Data:`
   - `[+] With your explicit permission, we collect coarse or precise device location to display active performers on the Stage Radar.`
4. **Engagement & Analytics:** Voluntary tipping records, song requests, leaderboard scores, and crash telemetry.

---

## 3. How We Use Information & AI Systems
1. **Service Delivery & Payouts:** Facilitating payments, verifying creator accounts, distributing band revenue splits.
2. `[+] AI-Assisted Profile & Moderation Screening:`
   - `[+] We use Google Gemini models to assist creators with profile formatting and screen for toxic user content. Automated systems do not make unilateral decisions to suspend accounts or deny payouts without human review.`

---

## 4. Third-Party Data Sharing & Stripe Privacy Roles
1. `[+] Payment Processing & Stripe Privacy Role:`
   - `[+] We share transaction data with Stripe, Inc. Stripe processes personal data in accordance with the [Stripe Privacy Policy](https://stripe.com/privacy) and acts as an independent controller for regulatory compliance, fraud mitigation, and underwriting.`
2. **Infrastructure & Hosting:** Google Cloud Platform / Firebase (ADC-authenticated multi-tenant Firestore).
3. **No Sale or Sharing of Personal Data:** Crowdbeats does not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

---

## 5. Granular Data Retention Schedule
- **Account Profiles:** Retained while account remains active; permanently purged 30 days post-deletion.
- **Financial & Tax Records:** Retained for seven (7) years in accordance with statutory IRS and AML requirements.
- **Security & Incident Audit Logs:** Retained for three (3) years.

---
