## 1. Purpose & Scope
This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") defines prohibited content and conduct across all Crowdbeats services, including user profiles, live session chats, song request notes, tipping messages, creator broadcasts, and venue posts.

Crowdbeats maintains a strict zero-tolerance policy toward dangerous, illegal, and harmful content. Violations result in immediate content removal, account suspension, permanent platform ban, and reporting to legal authorities where warranted.

---

## 2. Absolutely Prohibited Content (Zero-Tolerance)
The following content is strictly prohibited without exception:

### 2.1 Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) & Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (CSAE)
- Any image, text, audio, link, or depiction involving the sexual abuse or exploitation of minors.
- Any grooming behavior or sexual solicitation of individuals under the age of 18.
- **Enforcement:** Immediate permanent ban, device/IP hash blacklisting, content preservation, and mandatory report submission to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and relevant law enforcement.

### 2.2 Violent Extremism & Terrorism
- Depictions, praise, or promotion of terrorist organizations, violent extremist ideologies, mass violence, or hate groups.
- Direct threats of violence, incitement of imminent lawless action, or instructions on constructing weapons or explosives.

### 2.3 Non-Consensual Sexual Content & Intimate Imagery
- Distribution or threatening to distribute intimate, sexual, or sexually explicit recordings without unambiguous documented consent.
- Deepfake sexual imagery or non-consensual synthetic media.

### 2.4 Suicide, Self-Harm, & Dangerous Acts
- Content encouraging, depicting, or providing instructions on suicide, self-injury, or eating disorders.

### 2.5 Illegal Goods, Substances, & Fraud
- Sale or solicitation of controlled substances, firearms, unauthorized pharmaceuticals, or counterfeit currency.
- Money laundering, card testing, structuring, fraudulent schemes, phishing, or pyramid sales.

## 3. Platform Integrity & Respectful Conduct
Users and creators agree not to:
1. **Harass or Threaten:** Direct targeted abuse, doxxing, stalking, or hate speech targeting protected characteristics (race, ethnicity, religion, disability, gender, gender identity, or sexual orientation).
2. **Infringe Intellectual Property:** Upload, perform, or distribute copyrighted music, artwork, or trademarks without authorization from rights holders (see [DMCA Policy](/legal/dmca)).
3. **Manipulate System Integrity:** Deploy bots, scrape proprietary data, inject malicious scripts, exploit vulnerabilities, or engage in automated tipping manipulations.
4. **Circumvent Moderation:** Create alternate accounts to evade disciplinary suspensions or bans.

---

## 4. Moderation & Enforcement Protocols
Crowdbeats operates automated real-time scanning and human review pipelines:
- **Pre-Publication Filtering:** Automated text and media filters block high-confidence violations in live chat and tipping messages.
- **Human Moderation Review:** Flagged content is reviewed by trained moderators within target SLA windows.
- **Repeat Offender Policy:** Progressive discipline: (1) Formal warning & content removal, (2) 14-day monetization restriction, (3) 30-day suspension, (4) Permanent platform termination.
