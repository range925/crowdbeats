## 1. Scope & Commitment
Crowdbeats LLC ("Crowdbeats", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes the categories of personal information we collect, how that information is used, shared with our payment provider **Stripe, Inc.**, and the legal rights available to you under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CPRA), GDPR, and other statutory frameworks.

---

## 2. Categories of Information Collected
1. **User Identity & Account Data:** Legal name, email, chosen display name, avatar, and persona classifications (Fan, Artist, Band, Sponsor).
2. **Payment Card & Connected Account Data:**
   - Fan card data is tokenized directly by **Stripe, Inc.** Crowdbeats does not store raw credit card numbers or CVC security codes.
   - Performing musicians and band payout recipients submit KYC information (legal entity name, SSN/EIN, date of birth, photo ID, bank account details) directly to Stripe.
3. **Location & Stage Radar:** Device geolocation is collected solely with runtime permission to display nearby live musical performances.
4. **Platform Telemetry & Audit Logs:** IP addresses (hashed for fraud prevention), browser data, crash reports, and administrative audit logs.

---

## 3. Purposes of Processing & Generative AI Safeguards
- To facilitate live stage engagement, song voting, and voluntary tipping.
- To execute marketplace disbursements and split calculations via Stripe Connect.
- **AI Profile & Moderation Assistance:** We utilize Google Gemini AI models to assist creators with profile summaries and pre-screen content flags. **Automated systems do not have authority to unilaterally suspend users or withhold payouts without qualified human review.**

---

## 4. Subprocessors & Third-Party Disclosures
1. **Stripe, Inc. (Payment Processing & Identity):** Governed by the [Stripe Privacy Policy](https://stripe.com/privacy). Stripe acts as an independent controller for identity verification, regulatory tax reporting, and fraud risk monitoring.
2. **Google Cloud / Firebase (Hosting & Telemetry):** All application databases are encrypted at rest with AES-256 and transit encrypted with TLS 1.3.
3. **Affirmative No-Sale Pledge:** Crowdbeats does not sell or share personal information for third-party behavioral advertising.

---

## 5. Retention Schedule & Statutory Holds
- User profile data is deleted within thirty (30) days of account deletion, except where financial, tax, or fraud investigation records must be retained for seven (7) years under federal accounting laws.
