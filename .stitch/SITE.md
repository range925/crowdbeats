# Crowdbeats V2 — Site Architecture & Stitch Loop Roadmap

## 1. Vision & Overview
Crowdbeats is the premier direct-to-creator live music ecosystem:
- Fans discover real-time gigs, tip artists directly with 2 taps, and fund local concerts.
- Solo musicians check into venues, display interactive QR codes, and take home 94% net payouts with zero recurring subscription fees.
- Bands configure automated, instant revenue splits for all members.
- Venues discover vetted local talent and drive beverage/attendance sales.
